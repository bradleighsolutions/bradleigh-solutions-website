Where It All Started

29 Years in the Making

BradLeigh Solutions was built by a team of trucking industry veterans with a combined 50+ years of hands on experience moving vehicles across Western Canada and beyond. This is a team driven company, built on a foundation of solid, real world experience, and a simple promise behind every load: we treat your vehicle like it is our own.

That deep industry foundation translated directly into BradLeigh Solutions Ltd. and was established over 11 years ago to serve the growing demand for professional vehicle and car hauling services across Canada and into the United States. From day one, the focus has been simple: show up, do the job right, and be the kind of company people can count on.

Based in Alberta and licensed to operate on both sides of the border, BradLeigh has grown into a trusted name for dealerships, fleet operators, and commercial customers who need vehicles moved safely, efficiently, and on time.
